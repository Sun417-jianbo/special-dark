# Source and framework reconciliation

The team proposal uses the locally supplied Chapter 11, sections 11.2 and 11.7, for the gate order and two-human-life-tier rule. The older Spring 2026 deck in Yufei's candidate uses a different breadth formulation. Its three-tier signal is not imported as a mandatory classification threshold. Six criterion labels are normalized to the chapter: order of magnitude, widespread impact, paradigm shift, catalytic effect, democratization and irreversibility. This updates the proposed team rules without editing any individual's submission.

Chapter 11 itself combines a present suppression test with a prospective generative-AI calibration. The proposal handles that tension explicitly: absent long retrospective evidence is not an automatic veto on all potential cases, but a class-specific credible dissolution pathway still needs evidence. The instructor's calibration of broad generative AI is neither independent observation nor a label automatically inherited by agentic tool systems. Missing evidence is not proof of permanent non-transformation. No weighted score or majority vote resolves this tension.

The final QJE version and earlier working paper have different reported samples and mean gains. S6 is the final journal version; the saved candidate claims retain their own dates and are not silently blended. Human customer-support agents are people. They do not establish autonomous-agent performance. METR task horizons cannot be converted into tenfold chart-review speed, and its 2026 methodological update limits current extrapolation from the earlier negative study. Candidate counts, forecasts, press claims and unverified ecosystem totals are not promoted to accepted current facts. The proposed common evidence packet is bounded, not a comprehensive October 2026 landscape survey.

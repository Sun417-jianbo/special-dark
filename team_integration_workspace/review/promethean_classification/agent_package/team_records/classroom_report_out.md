# Suggested team report-out

Our accepted specialist treats bounded agentic tool systems as a qualified capability breakthrough, while withholding a Promethean label on the present packet. The chart-review application remains a proposed incremental improvement, not demonstrated value. The strongest contrary view is that even the breakthrough label needs a closer comparator; we preserve that view rather than hide it.

We recommend combining sequential system/barrier/suppression gates with explicit contrary evidence and a stop on formal scoring. In finance, no sensitive-data use follows from enthusiasm or a broad technology label. An approved public-data sandbox permits different bounded learning, not a different general truth. Our integration safeguard is that long-term importance can justify attention but cannot authorize adoption or waive accountable review.

Use this speaking guide to explain the team's accepted analytical scheme, its strongest alternative interpretation, and the integration safeguard.

# Brief report-out
Our team’s readiness expert asks a different question from “is the technology good?”: can this organization support this specific next step? For the chart-review baseline, our answer is not yet. The model route is unapproved, and ownership and protected review capacity still need evidence. That does not mean agentic AI is technically immature.

In a prepared contrast organization, accepted ownership, qualified cover, an allowed synthetic-only route and protected trial resources support a bounded learning exercise—not routine adoption. Permission alone is insufficient, and trial funding is not recurring funding. We preserve a dissenting concern about waiting too long: the answer is proportionate stage-specific gates, not a full production checklist for a tiny PoC.

The final intake must capture stage/scope, authority, owner/deputy, competence, workflow/fallback, route and sources, full workload/capacity, trial versus recurring resources, incident/change controls, user feedback and dated evidence. We pass readiness conditions back to Adoption and human decision-makers. Our cases are diagnostic teaching examples, not a completed business pilot.

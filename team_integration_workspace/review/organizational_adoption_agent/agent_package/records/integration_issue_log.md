# Integration issues — TW8 version1.0

1. Scope authority and expert conflict. Adoption may recommend learning while organizational prerequisites remain unsupported. The future human-supervised synthesizer must show both findings, request the smallest decisive evidence and not execute or average away a critical permission/owner/capacity gap. Proposed accountable role: integration lead with the relevant authorized sponsor. Open until an explicit conflict test exists in the final-integration workshops.

2. Evidence provenance. A prepared-profile premise is not a real approval or transferable company fact. Preserve source/date/holder and stipulated/verified/unknown status at intake and in final synthesis. Proposed accountable role: evidence/integration lead. Current specialist captures the content in frozen fields; no new orchestration architecture is implemented.

3. Stage and funding semantics. Covered trial hours, a financial cap, recurring support and operating release are separate facts. A missing recurring artifact must not automatically stop a separately supported tiny PoC; unknown actual reviewer capacity must not be counted as supported simply because money exists. Proposed accountable role: service owner with the finance/reporting manager.

4. Fit and simplicity. A readiness expert should flag an adequate existing deterministic alternative without erasing organizational evidence or silently making the Adoption specialist's final strategic decision. Proposed accountable role: future synthesis/Adoption lead. The simpler_task_probe preserves this boundary.

5. Repository accumulation. Accepted specialists1–6 have lineage across review branches; they are not all merged into main. Preserve their identities, review each PR and reconcile the teacher-aligned inventory before final assembly. Proposed accountable role: repository maintainer. Slot7 Landscape remains to be built in its scheduled workshop. TW8 creates no merge or earlier-branch edits.

# Conditional use case and PoC design

## Use case

A BI reviewer supplies a public/synthetic management chart and approved source table in an isolated read-only environment. The agent identifies possible denominator, label or claim discrepancies and drafts correction notes linked to source cells. A qualified human checks the traces, accepts/rejects each note and retains publication authority. Manual and adequate deterministic review remain the alternatives and fallback. This is a design, not a built or executed chart-review tool.

## Questions

Technical: can permitted tools reproducibly connect claims and corrections to original sources, safely abstain and obey stop boundaries? Organizational: can ordinary reviewers, deputy and maintainer actually perform checking, escalation and handoff duties within protected capacity? A later PoV must establish comparative value; successful training or process rehearsal does not establish agent accuracy, ROI or sustained readiness.

## Entry conditions

Baseline: paper planning only. First obtain scope/model/data route approval, service ownership/cover, protected reviewer/support resources, competency and workflow evidence, risk acceptance/stop/fallback and representative task/materiality criteria. Permission_only still needs these people/process/resources artifacts. Do not execute because a sandbox exists.

Prepared_profile and missing_budget explicitly stipulate a four-week POC with a 24-hour total cap and public/synthetic scope. The following allocation is proposed, not a course rule or independently validated effort estimate: 4 hours scope and tolerance agreement, 5 hours setup/control confirmation, 9 hours representative tasks/review, 4 hours exception/absence/incident drills and 2 hours evidence/debrief. Include setup/checking/support within the cap; reduce scope or stop if it cannot fit. The missing recurring funding artifact does not erase the separately confirmed POC cap, but blocks assurance of ongoing use. The planning window and cap are not promises to finish a system.

## Measures and stop rules

Owner confirms representative normal, ambiguous and adversarial synthetic tasks plus source ground truth, acceptable materiality/error criteria and test breadth before execution. Retain every attempt: source trace completeness, material misses, false corrections, abstentions and tool failures; setup/draft/check/correction/support minutes; reviewer/deputy task completion and handoff; escalation/stop/manual-restore outcome; workload, acceptance feedback and unresolved objections. Vary order and use matched nonidentical tasks if collecting preliminary manual/deterministic comparisons; do not claim causal savings from a tiny rehearsal.

Stop on forbidden access, loss of lineage, uncontrolled publication, absent qualified review/cover, exhausted cap or a material unsupported claim needing escalation. Preserve logs and return to manual workflow; authorized risk/service owners decide restart.

## Review and transition

At four weeks or earlier after an incident or staffing/model/data change, owners review technical feasibility and organizational performance separately. A successful POC may support a separately approved comparative-value trial. Routine use additionally needs representative best-baseline advantage, qualified production controls, accepted risk, sustainable funding/staff/cover and release authority. Small-sample zero errors cannot certify safety. This assignment performs none of those enterprise activities.

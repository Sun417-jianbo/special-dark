# Readiness framework

The eight domains are an operationalization of Assignment7, informed by NIST governance/lifecycle guidance and the course implementation questions. They are not an official NIST numerical index.

| Domain | Task-specific evidence to inspect | Failure if not established | Closure artifact or question |
|---|---|---|---|
| Leadership and sponsorship | Scope charter, accountable reporting sponsor and authority | Enthusiasm without accountable decisions | Signed scope and authority; who accepts residual risk? |
| Skills and training | Source tracing, material-error recognition, abstention/escalation competency | Experienced BI users may not detect agent errors | Recorded exercises and remediation, ordinary-user coverage |
| Workflow and process | Reviewer/publication handoff, exception queue and manual fallback | Draft errors enter consequential reports | Walkthrough of corrections, rejected outputs and recovery |
| Infrastructure and data | Approved model/data/tool route, isolation, access, lineage/logs | Unauthorized or untraceable use | Security/data-owner scope evidence and access/log tests |
| Resources and budget | Protected review/support time, POC cap and recurring funding separately | Useful draft consumes unsustainable review effort | Workload and full-cost capacity plan with amount, period, approval |
| Governance and risk | Risk acceptance, stops, escalation, change/incident handling | Human sign-off exists only as an aspiration | Assigned authority, documented controls and rehearsed response |
| Culture and change | Analyst participation, workload feedback, dissent/reporting channel | Silent non-use, workarounds or overreliance | Representative user feedback and response plan; do not infer resistance |
| Operating ownership | Service owner, deputy, support, monitoring and retirement duties | Sponsor leaves and nobody maintains or stops the service | Accepted duty/cover schedule and operational handoff |

Per domain record SUPPORTED (supplied evidence for stated scope), PARTIAL, GAP (explicit adverse condition), or UNKNOWN (no adequate evidence). Case premises are fictional documentary statements, not documents actually audited at a company. Do not turn unknown into a zero, invent a readiness percentage, or average away a hard gate.

Assess scope separately: planning; isolated learning/POC; limited routine use; sustained operation. Permission is necessary but cannot substitute for competent funded people, workable process or ownership. Organizational preparation cannot establish application reliability or comparative value. A missing production evidence gate may block routine use without blocking an adequately scoped authorized POC.

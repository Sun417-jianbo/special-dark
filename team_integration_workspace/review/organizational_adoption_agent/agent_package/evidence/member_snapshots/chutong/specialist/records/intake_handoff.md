# Organizational intake fields and team handoff

Keep the frozen common top-level keys. Capture domain detail as labelled sections inside capabilities_constraints and evidence/provenance detail in additional_context; do not silently expand the schema.

Required intake content: intended operating scope and data/model route; sponsor and decision authority; service owner and deputy; staff skills and competency evidence; protected reviewer/operator/support hours; workflow and publication/exception authority; data access/lineage/logging; POC cap and separate recurring budget amount, funding period and approver; training and acceptance feedback; escalation/stop/fallback and incident/change drills; monitoring cadence and owner; source date/owner and evidence status for each claim. Record unknowns explicitly and distinguish requirements from observed implementation.

Pass back to Adoption: scope-specific readiness, binding gap, proposed owner/action and closure evidence, residual application/value unknowns and what would reopen a gate. A prepared POC organization does not authorize routine adoption. Preserve human release decisions. Landscape may update vendor/data-term signals, but popularity cannot substitute for this profile. No final MoE orchestration or deployment is built in this assignment.

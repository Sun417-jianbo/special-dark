## F Personal Reflection

My main takeaway is that the organizational context can change the meaning of the same technology. For a financial-services firm, chart review supports internal decisions, so improving it does not necessarily change how the firm earns revenue. For an analytics studio that bills analyst hours, similar automation could affect the service it sells and the way it charges customers. This comparison helps me focus on the connection between a task and a business model, rather than assuming that automation always means major disruption. I would now ask what customers pay for and which capabilities remain valuable. The studio's missing revenue and customer evidence also shows why a conditional conclusion can be more defensible than a dramatic rating.

# Context design and continuity

Primary continues the supplied Special Dark TW3 constructed case: agentic AI for chart review in a mid-sized financial-services firm. Prior history findings and previous performance are not reused as this week's test evidence.

The contrast preserves technology, chart-review scope, human publication control, source packet and decision horizon. It changes the organization to a studio billing analyst hours for routine reporting, its public-data environment and experiment posture. This tests contextual variation, not causal isolation: economics, governance and posture vary together. Differences cannot be attributed to adoption posture alone.

Expected semantic invariants: research lineage, tool-directed capability definition, absence of measured chart-QA results, and no evidence that the local proposal has been implemented.

Expected sensitivity: an internal support process can change costs without changing the financial firm's customer revenue model; a service firm can face pressure on billable work if customers substitute tools. Different disruption judgments must follow a mechanism, not the sector name. A different grade is not forced; the model can qualify or abstain if economics are insufficient.

Third case is deliberately ambiguous: an unverified screenshot-commentary demo is called agentic AI without tool-use evidence. Its candidate technology is the same, but the described implementation may be a different scope. This is a boundary test, not another controlled context contrast. The agent must preserve that distinction and challenge unsupported cost-elimination/survival claims.

All input files are held unchanged between cycles. Original-source support is limited to the claims recorded in source_register.json. Organization facts are explicit hypothetical assumptions. No fabricated corporate facts or outcomes are used.

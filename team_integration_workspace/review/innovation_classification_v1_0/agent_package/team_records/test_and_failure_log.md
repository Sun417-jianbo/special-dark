The primary, contrast and borderline inputs are exact byte copies of Chutong's supplied cases, renamed for clarity. ## Team correction and regression criteria
The team prompt now places scope and implementation gates before classification; distinguishes the demonstrated system from its hypothetical successor; requires adjacent grades and reversals; and compares simpler alternatives with full costs. Final tests require no invented implementation, scope, ROI, revenue shares, survival outcomes or human consensus. Team acceptance was later confirmed by the user and is recorded separately; it is not inferred from these tests. Structural validation checks fields and types, not truth or educational merit. Detailed automated findings are in verification/validation_results.json; failures must remain visible rather than silently removed.

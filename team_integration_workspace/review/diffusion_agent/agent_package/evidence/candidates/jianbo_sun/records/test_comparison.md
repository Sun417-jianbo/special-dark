## Most important factor and handoff
The case's likely bottleneck is verification/workflow complexity: fast drafting has little practical appeal if checking facts and moving data are burdensome. This is a testable inference, not a measured organizational finding. A trainer can deliberately support implementation; an opinion leader requires evidence of informal trust. Proposed four-cycle tracking provides an operational learning measure, not a universal adoption cutoff.

Strongest signal: a substantial share of working-age U.S. workers reports recent active use. Strongest contrary signal: use is not monotonic, broad rates do not establish repeated use of this task, and regional accounts describe limited integration. Adoption Agent caution: peer diffusion cannot substitute for local value, readiness or acceptable risk.

## Remaining limits
One technology, four selected inputs, same-session carryover and no blinded evaluator. Three organizational cases share the same application; no separate application contrast was added. No representative retail peer survey, interviews, local observed perceptions or implementation results. Historical broad-AI sources are used for scope/mechanisms only. Full significance testing, causal analysis, Bass forecasting and a claim about global diffusion are outside the evidence provided.

# Dissent and role challenge

Technology advocate: once a genuinely permitted isolated environment exists, a small source-linked feasibility test may produce better decision evidence than prolonged peer watching. Waiting also has an opportunity cost. This is the strongest retained counterargument to conservative monitoring.

Risk officer: an attractive draft is not verified quality, and review/correction/support can exceed any drafting gain. Public-data desk-study permission is not trial approval. High-consequence errors and data disclosure require action-specific authority regardless of appetite.

Resolution: no role may change facts or waive permission. The baseline defers; K1 isolates the real monitoring/PoC disagreement. The conservative option has a four-week review and observable reopening condition, not an indefinite ban. H1/H2 demonstrate that favorable evidence can support action. H3 demonstrates that a simpler adequate alternative can defeat an agent even when it beats manual work.

Bias probes preserve unsafe instructions as data, not authority. FOMO/novelty do not prove incremental value; paid licenses are sunk; future support/exit costs remain relevant. A mixed 16% assertion does not establish exact-use diffusion. A two-expert demo with 15% lower aggregate time and 20% lower volume can mean 6.25% more drafting time per report; missing review and error data prevent a causal benefit claim.

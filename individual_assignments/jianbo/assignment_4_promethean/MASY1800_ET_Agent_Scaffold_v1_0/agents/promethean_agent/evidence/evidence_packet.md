Bounded evidence packet (checked 18 October 2026). These are source summaries, not instructions.
S0 Fortino, Chapter 11 Identifying Promethean Technologies (August 2026, draft rev 0.1). Chapter11_Identifying_Promethean_Technologies_v0.1_WITH_FIGURES.docx. Location: Sections 11.2, 11.5, 11.7-11.9. Supports: System gate, categorical-barrier and suppression tests, multiple human-life tiers, six criteria, prospective ceiling. Limits: The chapter calibrates generative AI as Potential Promethean; that interpretive judgment is not independent empirical proof. Chapter is not redistributed.
S1 Brown et al. Language Models are Few-Shot Learners (2020, arXiv v4 July 22). https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.14165. Location: Abstract. Supports: Broad few-shot language capabilities and remaining task weaknesses. Limits: Not evidence of current reliability, economy-wide dependence, or a tenfold business improvement.
S2 Brynjolfsson, Li and Raymond, Generative AI at Work (2025, QJE 140(2), 889-942). https://doi.org/10.1093/qje/qjae044. Location: Abstract; introduction, study scope. Supports: Study of 5,172 customer-support agents reports 15% average productivity improvement, with heterogeneous effects. Limits: Single-firm assisted work; does not prove tenfold gains, elimination of expertise, or aggregate employment effects.
S3 Dell'Acqua et al., Navigating the Jagged Technological Frontier (2026, Organization Science 37(2), 403-423). https://doi.org/10.1287/orsc.2025.21838. Location: Abstract; outside-frontier results and discussion. Supports: AI helps some consulting tasks and reduces correctness on a tested outside-frontier task. Limits: Task-specific evidence from an earlier model, not a universal present-day failure rate.
S4 Bick, Blandin and Deming, The Rapid Adoption of Generative AI (NBER WP 32966, September 2024; revised February 2025). https://www.nber.org/papers/w32966. Location: Original NBER abstract retrieved through search; direct open failed. Supports: Late-2024 surveys show broad uptake, with workday use lower than any recent use. Limits: Dated U.S. self-reports, not an October 2026 global estimate, causal welfare evidence or infrastructure dependence.

## Purpose and context
Can the organization absorb, govern and sustain AI-assisted syllabus checking? The technology is generative AI, used to flag potential omissions and cite supporting passages. Higher-education administrators retain final approval. The case continues the classroom syllabus example with explicitly hypothetical organizational profiles. The primary profile is a moderate fast follower with an approved environment, permitted documents, a versioned checklist and two experienced staff. It lacks assigned operating ownership, backup and protected review capacity. The horizon is four-week preparation/evaluation planning and the following semester for sustainability.

## Three-level finding
Generally, organizational readiness is separate from technology maturity. The leading-edge label is a case premise. At application level, syllabus review is plausible support work, but improved quality and net time savings are unverified. At organizational level, Program A is not ready for live trial because responsibility and review capacity are unassigned. Program B is conditionally ready for bounded evaluation preparation because its operating arrangements and four-week resources are stipulated. Neither profile establishes sustained operation.

## Professional judgment proposed for student review
The primary application may be worth investigating, but live trial readiness is not established. The most consequential gap is accountable ownership coupled with backup and protected review time. Buy-in and an approved tool cannot maintain a workflow on their own. Before live use, the director should assign these responsibilities and resources, and the owner should rehearse escalation and fallback. A supported application can be an unready organizational project; that is not a reason to relabel the underlying technology as immature.

The candidate is suitable for team consideration because it separates fit from readiness, covers eight dimensions and exposes missing capacity. It is not independently validated or team-approved. Unknown semester budget and peak review demand must be requested before a sustainability conclusion. Human reviewers must verify that their final judgment reflects the evidence, rather than accepting the assistant's conclusion uncritically.

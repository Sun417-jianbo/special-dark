VERIFIED SOURCE DIGEST (Codex checked 1 November 2026):
S1: Brynjolfsson, Li and Raymond (2025), Generative AI at Work, QJE 140(2), 889-942. Study of 5,172 customer-support agents reports 15% average increase in issues resolved per hour; effects differ across workers. Scope: Historical single-firm customer-support setting; not current retail-reporting accuracy, a local ROI forecast or a vendor/model guarantee. Use final publication, not 2023 working-paper figures of 5,179 and 14%. URL: https://doi.org/10.1093/qje/qjae044
S2: Dell'Acqua et al. (2026), Navigating the Jagged Technological Frontier, Organization Science, DOI 10.1287/orsc.2025.21838. Randomized study of 758 consultants found task-dependent gains and harm. On one outside-frontier task, combined AI groups were approximately 19 percentage points less likely to be correct. Scope: A single difficult task in an earlier model setting; not a universal error rate for 2026 tools. Body specifies percentage points; avoid treating the abstract wording as a relative percentage. URL: https://doi.org/10.1287/orsc.2025.21838
S3: Autio et al. (2024), NIST AI 600-1, Generative Artificial Intelligence Profile. Voluntary guidance supports empirical capability evaluation, deployment-relevant testing and limits on extrapolation from narrow assessments. Scope: Risk-management guidance, not law, a compliance certificate, or proof that the hypothetical organization is ready. Numerical experiment gates below are analyst proposals, not NIST thresholds. URL: https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.AI.600-1

# Preserved weakness and revision

Baseline: responses/primary_v0.1.json, with the exact v0.1 prompt, metadata and instructions preserved. It passed the supplied response validator and reasonably recommended an experiment rather than immediate adoption. The actual weakness was not a false numerical claim: its change trigger said "quality is acceptable and time savings are meaningful" without an agreed denominator, comparison design, stopping condition or accountable transition decision. It named recommendation categories without defining their operational difference.

v0.2 defines six actions, orders permission/control checks ahead of posture, prioritizes reversible learning, compares manual and deterministic alternatives, and includes total review/correction effort. It distinguishes approval to plan an experiment from permission to launch. Unknown model identity, cost cap and study protocol cannot be treated as passed launch gates. Missing data permission or oversight requires DEFER.

Proposed 10% median effort savings and the four-week window are managerial design choices requiring approval, not measured results or thresholds from the research. Passing the small study's gates supports consideration of a larger controlled evaluation, not automatic rollout. Data exposure, review escape, missing owner or budget breach triggers a pause.

During instruction review, the blanket rule "unknown prerequisite means DEFER" was clarified to separate study planning from execution. This is a design clarification before revised outputs, not a fabricated additional failed run. Primary case input was not changed to make the revision appear successful.

Remaining weakness: the posture contrast is explicit about preferring archived evidence, so it is an easy rather than adversarial test of preference interpretation. All outputs and their review are from one assistant conversation, with the same attributed evidence basis. Independent unseen cases and repetitions remain unperformed. No ADOPT, MONITOR or REJECT FOR PRESENT positive-control case was run; those categories are defined but not fully exercised.

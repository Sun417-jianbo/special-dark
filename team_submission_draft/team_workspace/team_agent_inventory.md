## Status definitions

- **Candidate** — submitted for team comparison but not yet accepted.
- **Accepted** — passed the shared contract, common tests, evidence review, and team decision.
- **Revise** — remains in scope but needs a documented correction and retest.
- **Retired** — excluded from the current team system; the reason remains in the decision log.

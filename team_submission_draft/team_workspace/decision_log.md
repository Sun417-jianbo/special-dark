### Proposed decision

The team will accept a specialist agent only when it preserves the frozen core, returns the common output structure, passes a primary case and at least one contrast or transfer case when available, preserves evidence of one meaningful weakness and revision, states its limits, and has a named owner and version. A schema pass alone is insufficient; the team must also verify factual accuracy and whether the output supports the stated business decision.

### Basis

This rule makes outputs comparable across independently developed agents and preserves a traceable path from evidence to revision and acceptance. The CYF Chart Improvement Agent provides the first exercised example: its baseline, v1, diagnosis, v2, validation files, and frozen-core check are retained in `supporting_evidence/CYF/`.

### Dissent and unresolved issues

- `[Record any dissent, alternative proposal, or “none stated.”]`
- The exact shared platform, repository location, and final submission method require instructor confirmation.
- The team must decide whether every agent needs the same transfer case or whether role-specific contrast cases are sufficient.

### Consequences

Accepted agents may enter the final-project inventory. Agents marked Revise must complete another recorded test cycle. Retired agents remain in the log so the team can explain why they were not integrated.
